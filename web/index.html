TVL over time (USD)

USD = supply × NAV × ECB TRY/USD daily rate. iTRY TVL is the entire circulating iTRY priced at NAV; wiTRY TVL is just the iTRY held inside the vault.

TVL over time (TRY)

TRY = supply × NAV. Same series as the USD chart above, but priced in lira instead of dollars — useful for spotting protocol-level growth independent of FX moves.
