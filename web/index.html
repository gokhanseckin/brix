1 wiTRY share value over time

Left axis: iTRY per share (totalAssets / totalSupply). Right axis: USD per share (left × NAV × ECB TRY/USD rate). NAV is currently pegged at 1.0 TRY per iTRY, so the USD line tracks the lira's value against the dollar.

iTRY supply & TVL over time (TRY)

iTRY supply = all minted iTRY × NAV. iTRY TVL = iTRY currently locked inside the wiTRY vault. Lira pricing reflects protocol growth independent of FX moves.

iTRY supply & TVL over time (USD)

Same series as the TRY chart above, repriced through the ECB daily TRY/USD reference rate.

Primary iTRY issuance

Fresh iTRY minted in transactions that did not route into the wiTRY vault — i.e. issuance to user wallets and operators rather than vault yield. Bars show daily issuance (left axis), line shows cumulative iTRY issued since the chart start (right axis).

Yield delivered to wiTRY vault

On-chain proof of yield: any iTRY transferred into the vault in a transaction that did not mint wiTRY shares is pure yield, since it raises totalAssets without changing totalSupply. Bars show daily delivery (left axis), line shows cumulative iTRY delivered since launch (right axis).
